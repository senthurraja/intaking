1Intake — three directions, each tappable

Assumptions I designed against: one 2,200 kcal goal, full macros, manual entry after each meal, barcode as the fast path and a photo as the fallback with an honest range rather than a fake single number. Handwriting is used for figures and margin notes only — the chrome stays in the system's serif. Tap the bottom tabs and the buttons inside each phone: Today → Scan → Confirm, and Photo from the Today screen. Onboarding, weekly summary, food detail and settings come with whichever direction you pick.
